Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android application Sama Tools handles information. It explains what is processed locally on your device and what may be processed by third-party services, such as Google AdMob.

1. Introduction

Sama Tools ("we", "our", "the app") is a utility and tools application for Android. The app offers a set of everyday tools that process your content directly on your device.

Sama Tools does not operate its own backend server or database. The app does not require you to create an account or register, and it does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, password, contacts, precise location, health information, financial information, or the files you process, and store it on developer servers.

By using Sama Tools, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Please read it carefully.

2. Information We Collect

Sama Tools itself does not collect or transmit your personal information to the developer. Because the app has no backend server and no internal database, there is no mechanism for the developer to receive or store the content you process with the app.

However, the app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob and its partners may collect or process certain device, advertising, diagnostic, or usage information according to their own policies. This is described in more detail in the Advertising and Google AdMob and Third-Party Services sections.

3. Information Processed Locally on the Device

The tools provided by Sama Tools, such as PDF tools, file converters, compression tools, and similar utilities, perform their processing locally on your device. The files and data you select are used only to perform the operation you request.

Any files or data processed by these local tools remain on your device. Sama Tools does not upload them to the developer, and it does not store them on developer servers. The results of a tool are saved to your device, or shared using your device’s system sharing features, only when you explicitly choose to do so.

Some features, such as sharing or exporting, use your device’s operating system. Your files are only transmitted elsewhere if a specific feature explicitly sends data to a third-party service, and only when you take action that triggers that transmission.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

The current version of Sama Tools uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. Advertising is a third-party service, separate from the tools of Sama Tools itself.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or process certain information about your device and your interaction with ads, including device identifiers, advertising IDs, approximate location, diagnostic data, and usage data, in accordance with their own privacy and advertising policies.

Because advertising is provided by Google, the developer does not control the specific data that Google collects in connection with ads shown in the app. Please review the applicable Google policies for details:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Advertising Policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You may also be able to limit or reset advertising identifiers using the settings provided by your device’s operating system.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party services for specific functions:

  • Google AdMob — provides in-app advertising. AdMob operates under Google’s privacy and advertising policies, as described in the Advertising section.
  • Device system services — standard functions such as saving files to your device storage, selecting files, and sharing content use the capabilities of your Android device’s operating system.

Each third-party service operates according to its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party service you interact with.

6. How We Use Information

Sama Tools itself uses the content you provide only to perform the tool operation you request, entirely on your device. The developer does not use, review, or store your content because the app has no backend to receive it.

Any information processed by Google AdMob is used by Google for advertising-related purposes in accordance with Google’s policies, which are linked above.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Data and files processed by Sama Tools are stored locally on your device and are retained there until you delete them. The developer does not store your data because the app has no server or cloud database.

Data collected by third-party services, including Google AdMob, is stored and retained by those services according to their own policies.

8. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Because Sama Tools does not collect or store personal data on developer servers, there is no personal data for the developer to share.

Data may be shared with third parties only in the following cases:

  • When you intentionally share or export a file using your device’s system sharing features.
  • When Google AdMob processes advertising data as described in its own policies.
  • When disclosure is required by applicable law or legal process.

9. Children’s Privacy

Sama Tools is not directed to children under the age of 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising served through Google AdMob is subject to Google’s own policies regarding children’s privacy.

If you believe a child has provided information through a third-party service used by the app, please contact us using the details below.

10. Security

Because the app processes content locally, your files are not transmitted to or stored on developer servers, which reduces the risk associated with centralized storage. The security of your device itself is your responsibility; we recommend using the security features provided by your device’s operating system, such as a screen lock.

The security of information handled by third-party services, including Google AdMob, is governed by those services’ own security practices.

11. User Rights and Choices

Because Sama Tools does not collect or store personal data on developer servers, there is no personal data held by the developer for you to access, correct, or delete.

Your rights regarding data handled by third-party services, such as Google AdMob, are defined by those services. For example, you may be able to reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings. You can learn more about available choices in Google’s advertising policy linked in the Advertising section.

If you have questions or requests related to your privacy, you can contact us at any time using the contact details below, and we will do our best to assist you.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Any changes take effect when the updated policy is posted. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how the app handles information.
